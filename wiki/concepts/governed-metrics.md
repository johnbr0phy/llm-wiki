# Governed Metrics

**Last updated:** 2026-06-06

**TLDR:** Caliper's core pattern — versioned, auditable metric definitions with changelog and compliance exports.

## Summary

Governed metrics are Caliper's product thesis: business metrics defined in YAML (or UI), versioned over time, compiled to warehouse SQL, and exposed with a full audit trail. This is how Caliper differentiates from charting tools and ad-hoc spreadsheets.

Enterprise customers (e.g. Northwind Logistics) buy Atlas specifically so finance can audit the number. Maya Chen directed external messaging to say "governed metrics platform" rather than "analytics dashboard."
