Two layers, one source of truth

Your source material stays untouched. The LLM owns the compiled layer you read and query.

📥

raw/

Your source material. Transcripts, emails, docs. Never edited by the LLM — it's the ground truth.

🗂️

wiki/

LLM-compiled articles. Structured, interlinked, searchable. The LLM maintains this — you read it.

Three operations

The whole system runs on a simple loop: ingest, query, lint.

1

Ingest

Drop a new file in raw/, run the compiler, and the wiki updates incrementally.

2

Query

Ask a question. The LLM reads the wiki and gives you a sourced, cited answer.

3

Lint

Periodic health checks find contradictions, stale content, and broken links.

Five article types

The wiki organizes everything it learns into interlinked sections.

🚀

Projects

Initiatives with status, ownership, decisions, open questions, and timeline.

👤

People

Cross-project profiles: what each person owns and their recent activity.

⚖️

Decisions

Date-stamped records of significant choices — what, why, and what it affected.

💡

Concepts

Patterns and ideas that span multiple projects.

🗓️

Timeline

Quarterly summaries of what happened versus what was planned.

Get started in minutes

Open the folder in Claude Code or Cowork, drop in your first files, and compile.

# 1. Drop source material into the right folder
raw/calls/2025-06-15_Sprint-Review_Summary.md
raw/email/2025-06-16_Weekly-Digest.md
raw/docs/2025-06-10_Product-Spec.md

# 2. Tell Claude
"compile the wiki"

# 3. Ask anything
"What's the current state of Project X?"
"What has Sarah been involved in this quarter?"
"What's on my plate this week?"
